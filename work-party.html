Week 42 · Autumn

Six volunteers, five hours, one degraded wood

It's a mild October Saturday at Hollow Copse. The crew has signed in at the meet point by the gate, the tea urn is on, and there's far more work in the wood than one day can cover.

  • Tap volunteers to select them (tap again to drop one), or drag a box around a group. Their cards on the right work too.
  • Then tap a job (a plant, tree or pile) to give it to them, or tap open ground to send them there. They are deselected once they have their orders.
  • With nobody selected, tap anything to look at it. Press and hold to look at something while volunteers are selected. Tap the woodland floor to see what grows there.
  • Zoom in with the scroll wheel, a trackpad pinch or the + and − buttons; move around by right-click dragging or with the arrow keys (on a touchscreen, pinch and drag with two fingers). Show jobs rings everything still to do.
  • Select volunteers by dragging a box around them, clicking one, or using their cards on the right. Hold Shift to add to a group.
  • Right-click a plant, tree or pile to give your selected volunteers a job, or right-click open ground to send them there. They are deselected once they have their orders. On a touchscreen, press and hold instead.
  • Left-click anything to look at it. A left-click also clears your selection. If nobody is selected, the nearest free volunteer takes the job.
  • Tasks take time. Cut material is carried back to the meet point: invasive brash goes on the fire, hazel and other produce is bundled to sell, and rubbish goes in the skip.
  • Light brings flowers. Ground you open up fills with woodland flowers over the following seasons, unless bramble gets there first.
  • Left-click the woodland floor to see what grows there and how much light reaches it. The Canopy and Light map buttons let you look through the layers.
  • Some shrubs look alike. Volunteers with a good eye for plants will spot a mistake before they make it; others won't.

At 15:00 the tools go back in the trailer and you get a condition report for the compartment.
